A currency converter built for travel: point your camera at a price, convert any price from any app, and keep the local rate on your Lock Screen.

• Price scanner
Point the camera at a price tag or a menu. Cento recognizes prices on your iPhone and lays the converted amount right over the original. It reads currency symbols and codes (€, £, ₺, THB…) automatically. Recognition runs fully on device — images are never saved or sent anywhere.

• Convert from any app
Select a price in Safari, Notes, Mail or a messenger, tap Share → Convert in Cento, and see it in all your currencies without leaving the app you’re in.

• Travel rate on your Lock Screen
Abroad, a Live Activity shows the local currency in your home currency on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with ready amounts like 100, 1,000 and 10,000. Or pin any pair you want in Settings.

• Widgets
Exchange rate, your currency list, rate charts, crypto market, a Travel widget for the country you’re in, and a Scan Prices button with a quick cheat sheet.

• Many currencies at once
Type once and see the amount in every currency on your list, with fiat and crypto side by side.

• Built-in calculator
Add, subtract, multiply, divide and take percentages right in the converter: type 120 × 3 + 15% and see the result everywhere.

• Make it yours
16 hand-crafted app icons, light and dark themes, number formats and 14 languages.

Rates work offline from the last update, and your local currency is added automatically from your location.
